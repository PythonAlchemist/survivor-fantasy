## Research notes
- **No pre-game press interviews this season.** CBS blocked them. Primary first-person source
  is the full EW/CBS bio questionnaire (mirrored on the Survivor Fandom wiki and True Dork
  Times), which is far richer than the short press-release blurbs — 12 questions including
  "accomplishment you're most proud of," "something we'd never know from looking at you," and
  "biggest inspiration." Those three questions did most of the work below.
- The Fandom wiki's castaway table has the **tribe column blank**, independently confirming
  tribes are not public. Buffs are handed out on the beach, and one player leaves immediately
  on a "mysterious journey."
- **Shot in the Dark is confirmed in play.**
- **11 women, 10 men** — the first season ever with more women than men. First odd-numbered
  start since *Fiji* (S14), and the first season to do it intentionally. First two-tribe start
  since *Winners at War*.
- Rumored-cast reporting (spring 2026) used some different names/ages: Jenna Doore appeared as
  "Jenna Greenawalt," Sharonda Cox as "Sharonda Renee" (still her IG handle). CBS/EW is
  authoritative.

## TIER A — Draft first

### Mike Pinsky, 33 · Baseball executive · New York, NY
**Vitals:** **5'8".** Princeton psychology. Assistant Director of Baseball Operations, **New
York Yankees** — front office since Jan 2017, current role since Jan 2022, advising GM Brian
Cashman on roster construction and contract negotiations. Interned with the Nationals in 2016.
Married to Becky, his high school crush (they met acting in school plays; first stage kiss was
*A Midsummer Night's Dream*). Dachshund named Pookie. Home cook, pickup basketball, tennis,
improv comedy.
**Superfan:** Played Survivor with friends in NYC apartments at age 8; sacred Wednesday-night
college viewings; proposed to Becky using a Probst catchphrase.
**Backstory:** Lost his mother to metastatic breast cancer when he was 24; she was 61. Quotes
her line "everyone has something to offer" as his Day One mantra.
**Bio signals:** "Enthusiastic, strategic, driven." Comps: Kyle Fraser, Charlie Davis, Adam
Klein — but explicitly wants to be "this season's Rachel LaMont (the best new era winner)."
Alliance value is **pure intel-gathering**: "As in baseball front offices, information is
king… who's working with whom; who has what advantages; what was that whispered conversation
at the water well all about?" Self-scout: "I'm athletic, but at 5'8" I'm not physically
imposing. I'm strategic, but I know how to downplay it. And I'm a good chat without being a
charm machine who will be seen as too big a social threat."
**Fantasy read:** That self-scout is the single most calibrated paragraph any castaway wrote —
it's a threat-management thesis, and it's correct. Negotiation is literally his job. 5'8" and
unassuming solves the physical-threat problem that sinks half this cast. The one risk is the
occupation reveal: "Yankees executive" can read as rich and connected. Grief-driven motivation
plus an articulate delivery is also a strong jury package.
**Projection:** Highest floor *and* highest ceiling. **Top overall pick.**

### Jelly Loblack, 30 · Sociology professor · Bloomington, IN
**Vitals:** **PhD in Sociology earned before 30**, now a professor at **Indiana University**;
research on gendered racial socialization and anti-Black racialization. Trained in
**ethnographic interviewing and observation**. Afro-Caribbean and **Choctaw** descent — one of
only five castaways in show history with documented Native American ancestry. Volleyball,
running, debate. Once won "most comedic mascot" at mascot camp as Webber the Bear.
**Backstory:** Her grandmother — queer in an era when that meant risking imprisonment,
hospitalization, or losing your children; two-time breast cancer survivor and widow — is her
stated inspiration. Wants to win while the family who introduced her to the show is still here.
**Bio signals:** "Curious, multifaceted, passionate." Identifies with Katurah Topps; expects to
play like **Shan (S41), "for better or worse"** — she knows that comp is a warning. Wants an
ally "less ego-driven than myself." Closer: "I'm a driven, passionate brainiac in an
unsuspecting, beautifully wrapped package… There's nobody better equipped to win a social
experiment than the sociologist who creates them."
**Fantasy read:** Ethnographic training is not a gimmick — reading group dynamics from inside
a group *is* the job, and no one else here has formal training in it. The "unsuspecting
package" line says she plans to underplay early. The self-aware Shan comp is both encouraging
(she sees the failure mode) and worrying (she may still walk into it). Physical game is thin
for a 26-day sprint.
**Projection:** Merge lock on a healthy tribe; genuine winner equity.

### Alexis Levine, 34 · Criminal defense attorney · Atlanta, GA
**Vitals:** Emory Law JD; Univ. of Utah mass communication. Practice focused on mass
incarceration and marijuana legalization advocacy. **Ten years sober from drugs and alcohol** —
the accomplishment she names first. Iranian father who emigrated at 18 and never returned;
she's never been. Husband, daughter Lyla, two poodles. Gardening, reading, long walks.
**Bio signals:** "Outgoing, thoughtful, determined." Comps: Kyle Fraser "with a touch of
Rachel, and of course, Parvati." On preparation: "defending the little guy from the
all-powerful government means constantly being **'on the bottom'** and how to think
creatively." Alliance value: someone who "thinks and plays differently than me — diversity is
the best asset." Pet peeves: people ruled by their emotions, and misogyny.
**Fantasy read:** The "I'm a professional at playing from the bottom" answer is the most
useful thing anyone said about their own preparation — being down 4-2 after a tribe swap is
the modal Survivor situation, and she does that for a living. Coalition-building across
difference is the winner's answer to the alliance question. Ten years of sobriety signals
serious discipline under stress, and it's a genuine jury story. "Attorney" is a mild flag, but
defense reads warmer than prosecution.
**Projection:** Strong contender. Tier A on social/strategic upside.

### Carter Krull, 25 · Livestock farmer · Sioux Falls, SD
**Vitals:** Family's Moon Creek Farms cattle ranch, Rock Rapids, IA. University of Sioux Falls;
played **wide receiver and kicker**. Serial small-business guy: started a **snow cone truck**,
then co-founded a moving company that now relocates people nationwide (named, after extensive
deliberation, **"MOVE MOVE MOVE!"**). Woodworker — makes epoxy river tables, taught by his
grandfather and his high school shop teacher.
**Bio signals:** "Adventurous, competitive, determined." Blends Rob, Ozzy, Tony, and Sam
Phalen "depending on circumstances — the game is so situational." Alliance value: "The bottom
layer of flooring in this game is trust. Let's keep each other in the loop." Closer: "It's
gotta be one of us roundheads… why not me?"
**Fantasy read:** The archetype that keeps working — young, likable, physically useful, real
camp skills, not read as a mastermind. Farm labor plus college receiver is a legitimate
challenge asset without the bodybuilder silhouette that gets you cut at final 8. Two
businesses started before 25 says the affability hides real drive. "The game is so
situational" is a more sophisticated answer than it looks.
**Projection:** High floor. Provider role protects him pre-merge; threat risk only arrives if
he starts winning individual immunities.

## TIER B — Solid value

### Eric Macksoud, 34 · Mental health counselor · Windsor Locks, CT ⬆ *upgraded*
**Vitals:** Lincoln, RI native. Licensed LMHC — **passed the exam on his 4th attempt** after
refusing to quit, and cites it as his proof-of-resilience story. **Competitive swimmer from age
8 through his senior year of college** (Clark University Swim & Dive) **and trained in Muay
Thai kickboxing** — his own "you'd never guess" answer, because "I don't present as the
athletic type." Community theater (*Matilda*, *Rock of Ages*), electric guitar, horror movies,
cats. Engaged to Gina DeVito.
**Bio signals:** "Animated, charismatic, empathic." Comps to Domenick Abbate — "a charming,
slightly sassy East Coast Italian American like me." On preparation: community theater drops
him into a new group of mixed ages and backgrounds who must bond fast and perform — "this will
translate perfectly once I'm dropped off in a new tribe." Wants an ally who doubles as a
confidant. Pet peeves include low emotional intelligence and "super machismo type guys."
**Fantasy read:** **The most underrated player on the cast.** A decade-plus competitive swimmer
is enormous in Fiji, where a huge share of challenges are water-based — and he is *disguised*
as a non-athlete, which is the best possible combination. Add Muay Thai conditioning, a
therapist's read on people, and a performer's ability to bond fast. Domenick is a
one-vote-from-winning comp and an apt one. Risk: therapists sometimes read as *analyzing* you.
**Projection:** Quiet deep run. **Best value pick in the draft** — he'll go later than he
should.

### Sharonda Cox, 34 · OB-GYN resident · Richmond, KY
**Vitals:** Pompano Beach, FL; raised in a single-parent household. St. George's University
School of Medicine (2023). Advocate for Black women in medicine. Married. **First chair in
orchestra — played violin at Disney**; self-described science nerd and NASA kid. Fishing,
reading, watching sports with her husband.
**Backstory:** Maternal grandparents, who dropped out of school to work the bean fields, are
her stated inspiration — "I owe everything to them." Wants to show "little Black boys and
girls… what we can do despite our disadvantaged beginnings."
**Bio signals:** "Charismatic, resilient, reliable." Identifies with Cirie — "I am shocked by
the things I am able to accomplish. She is also a strong Black woman." Alliance value, the
sharpest answer on the cast: "**Loyalty but adaptability. If plans change 5 minutes before
tribal, you need to be both and ready to make it happen… otherwise, you are next!**" Closer:
"There isn't one thing this game can throw at me that I haven't already endured."
**Fantasy read:** That alliance answer is a top-3 response in the whole packet — it describes
how modern Survivor actually functions, and most players don't figure it out until they're
blindsided. Physician = camp provider *and* instant credibility. Countervailing: "doctor" plus
visible competence prices her as a merge threat, and residency-level exhaustion training is
either an asset or a warning depending on how depleted she arrives.
**Projection:** Merge lock, real winner equity. Best value if she slides past pick 6.

### Jenna Doore, 31 · Wedding photographer · Toledo, OH
**Vitals:** Perrysburg, OH. Bought her first camera at 15 with babysitting money; shot her
first wedding at 18; now a top-five-ranked wedding photographer in Ohio. Poet. Family nickname:
**"NENERGY."** Turned 31 on Day 10 of filming.
**Superfan:** Elite tier. Watching since age 7 — negotiated a later bedtime for the finale by
inventing her own immunity challenge (a one-minute handstand) and grinding at it for hours
until she could do it. **Every wedding contract she has sent for five years contains a
"Survivor clause"** voiding her services if she got cast. Wrote a poem about her dream and
gave away **154 copies**, each in exchange for the recipient telling her *their* dream — "I
have an army of people believing in it with me. I'm playing for them."
**Bio signals:** "Empathetic. Determined. Energetic." Wants **Charlie's** game (keep options
open, let yourself be underestimated, finger on the pulse) plus **Kenzie's** social range —
two of the best recent comps available. Alliance value: "Honestly — vibes… green flags if
they are a safe space to chat through strategic options." Self-aware: "I give off 'sweet,
bubbly, artistic girl'… but I've got a savvy strategic side."
**Fantasy read:** Her actual job is producing calm and rapport under high stakes from strangers
on the most emotionally charged day of their lives — that is a genuinely elite Survivor skill
set, and she knows the "underestimated" angle is her lane. The 154-poems story is a jury
speech waiting to happen. Risk is standard superfan overeagerness in the first 72 hours.
**Projection:** Good merge odds, top-tier jury-management upside.

### Kristin Flickinger, 49 · Crisis management · Santa Barbara, CA
**Vitals:** Oldest player on the cast. JD from Willamette University School of Law. Executive
Director, Pacific Pride Foundation 2020–2024; Interim ED, San Diego Pride. Career LGBTQ+
organizer since a 1990s National Gay & Lesbian Task Force internship; ran a field office during
the 2000s marriage campaigns; **raised $20M+ for HIV services and care**. Now runs Flickinger
Consulting, a crisis-management firm. Road cyclist. Trains her husky to talk with buttons.
Proudest of creating an **intergenerational culinary arts program for LGBTQ+ youth and
seniors** that reached thousands. Carries rocks in her bra "for protection… it changes based
on my mood."
**Superfan:** Applying **since the middle of Season 1** — 25 years. One of her three listed
hobbies is literally "applying for *Survivor* (I'm on the hunt for a new hobby…)."
**Bio signals:** "Determined, joyful." Comps to Rachel LaMont — "the first player who played
the way I think about the game." Alliance value: someone she can "know in my bones wants to go
to the end with me… also someone who wants to get up to mischief together. Papaya bandits,
anyone?" Why she'll win: her Aries sun and Scorpio rising. (A deliberate soft-pedal — the woman
who spent 25 years applying did not get cast on astrology.)
**Fantasy read:** Professionally trained in the exact thing Survivor tests — steering people
through catastrophe — on top of two decades of coalition organizing and fundraising, which is
persuasion at scale. The intergenerational program detail matters: she is demonstrably good at
getting young people and old people to like each other, which is the central pre-merge problem
for a 49-year-old. That's the whole risk: on a 26-day sprint with two tribes and a median age
of 30, older women are the archetypal early boot if the tribe loses twice.
**Projection:** Highest variance in Tier B. Survive two tribals and she goes deep. Boom-or-bust.

### Brady Booker, 27 · Pro wrestler · Knoxville, TN
**Vitals:** La Salle, IL. **D1 linebacker at the University of South Dakota** (political
science). Signed to **WWE NXT 2021–22 as "Bodhi Hayward,"** an original member of Chase U
alongside Andre Chase and Thea Hail; released 2022. Since: AEW Dark, regular Ring of Honor TV
tapings (including teaming with Billy Gunn), and currently the **reigning 1FW Heavyweight
Champion**. **Competitive gymnast throughout youth sports.** Hunting, fishing, hiking, camping,
weightlifting, trail running, jujitsu.
**Bio signals:** "Enthusiastic, intense, fun." Wants to play like Jonathan Young — "the
laid-back provider, until it is time to STRIKE!" On preparation: his job "involves long days,
minimal sleep and food, along with being able to politick your own social game" — a genuinely
sharp read on what wrestling and Survivor share. Alliance value: trust. Closer: "I am
comfortable being uncomfortable. I am used to falling on my face and coming back stronger."
**Fantasy read:** The most physically credentialed player here — D1 linebacker *plus* pro
wrestler *plus* trained gymnast means strength, balance, and obstacle work all at once. The
touring-wrestler point about sleep deprivation and locker-room politics is real preparation,
not a talking point. But Jonathan Young's game — dominate challenges, get cut at 6 — is the
honest projection, and Brady chose that comp himself.
**Projection:** Near-lock for merge on challenge value; ceiling capped by the threat label.
**Draft for challenge points, not for the win.**

### Ori Jean-Charles, 27 · Personal trainer · Spring Valley, NY
**Vitals:** 6'3", ~230 lbs. D1 linebacker — **transferred from Louisville to Maine** to be
closer to his injured mother, per his own account; **broke an NCAA Division I record** that
season and collected awards. Drove 8 hours home every weekend in an Audi A3 he could barely
fit in, for a semester and a half, until the car died. Pursued the NFL. Now a model, fitness
coach, and **Youth Services Director at the MLK Center** in Rockland County; nicknamed
"Coach O." **First in his family to earn a bachelor's *and* a master's (MPA)** — filled out
every form himself, sent money home rather than asking for it, and every sibling has since
graduated: "It's like I broke a generational curse." Played violin, first chair in 6th grade.
**Bio signals:** "Consistent, anomaly, hustler." Identifies with **Jeremy Collins** — strategic
play driven by family. Alliance value: "people that are doers and not scared… It's like
basketball: if I pass you the ball, SHOOT! Don't hesitate. Because if you don't, I might not
pass it to you again." Pet peeves: idiocy, and people who need attention.
**Fantasy read:** The physical ceiling of the cast and the most emotionally compelling
narrative in the packet — a jury would reward that story. But 6'3"/230 with no Edge, no
Redemption, and only 26 days is the textbook post-merge target, and his alliance answer
("shoot or I stop passing") signals low tolerance for hesitant partners, which creates friction
with the cautious players who tend to survive.
**Projection:** Challenge-points machine pre-merge; vulnerable the moment tribes dissolve.

### Linnea Capobianco, 26 · Entrepreneur · Jersey City, NJ ⬆ *upgraded*
**Vitals:** Kearny, NJ. **Co-owns Afterglow, a sex shop in NYC, with her brother.** **Studied
and worked in Washington, D.C.** — she cites political networking as her direct Survivor
preparation. High school soccer and lacrosse; now does Lagree (megaformer pilates). Street
photography, jazz shows. Inspiration: **Lynsey Addario**, the conflict/war photographer. Has
uncontrollable laughing fits at exactly the wrong moments. IG locked for the season.
**Bio signals:** "Rational, outgoing, optimistic." Comps to **Kyle Fraser** — "his ability to
build strong relationships and lead votes without being the over leader… extremely well
rounded (athletic and social strengths)." Alliance values, in her own ranked order: "True
friendship, positive attitude, doesn't freak out / get overly paranoid, **rational (most
important)**." Closer: "reading people, persuading people, and being a leader that doesn't come
off as threatening."
**Fantasy read:** Quietly one of the two or three sharpest bios here. A Kyle Fraser comp, an
explicit anti-paranoia alliance filter, and "leader who doesn't seem threatening" is a winner's
temperament stated three different ways. The D.C. networking answer is more concrete
preparation than most of the cast offered. Preseason analysts pegged her as stubborn based on
a photo; her actual answers say the opposite. Running a sex shop means she is unembarrassable
and good at putting nervous strangers at ease — underrated social utility.
**Projection:** Tier A game likely available at a Tier B/C draft slot. **Best value pick if
she falls past pick 8.**

## TIER C — Volatile / upside swings

### Danny "Kilby" Kilby, 30 · Game designer · London, ON ⚠️
**Vitals:** Mount Forest, ON. Marketing coordinator; founder of **Noodle Goose Games**.
**Co-hosts "The Winner's Edit," a Survivor podcast.** **Competed on season 2 of *Sequester
Access* in 2018, placing 18th** — he has actual prior reality-competition experience — and has
played numerous other ORGs. Met his girlfriend, who lives in Florida, **playing a fan-made
Survivor game**; says all his best friends came from the Survivor fandom. Student government
background. **First in his family to graduate high school and university** (straight A's); his
mother, a teen mom who raised him, then earned her own degree after he did — "her success is my
proudest accomplishment." Hobby list runs 14 deep, including Blood on the Clocktower, improv,
Lego, dodgeball, and WWE events.
**Bio signals:** "Wacky, chaotic, playful." Identifies with Todd Herzog or **Ben Katzman** —
"when 46 aired I got 9000 messages of people telling me I remind them of Ben." Wants to play
like **David Wright**: "aggressive, bold, moves built on empathetic, deep bonds." Alliance
value, said out loud: "I want to know someone's **'real laugh'** before I lock in, so I know
when they're lying in the future… Someone I can emotionally manipulate if I need to (sorry!)."
Also: "I come off as a happy go lucky, oddball hippie, and that's true, but I've suffered a
lot."
**Fantasy read:** The most Survivor-literate person in the cast by a wide margin, and that is
both his edge and his problem. The "real laugh" tell is a genuinely clever, professional-grade
read technique. Game-designer puzzle value is real. **But**: a podcast-hosting ORG veteran in a
75%-superfan cast is the profile that gets voted out first if anyone clocks him — and if he
plays David Wright's aggressive game, he'll be clocked. Mitigating factor discovered in the
social audit: **his personal reach is only 1.8K followers**, so most castmates genuinely won't
know him unless he tells them. Whether he can keep his mouth shut is the entire question.
**Projection:** Bimodal. Pre-merge flameout, or a deep, loud, big-move run. No middle outcome.

### Ana Sani, 35 · Voice actress · Toronto, ON
**Vitals:** Richmond Hill, ON. **Canadian Screen Award winner**; voice work in **60+ animated
shows** plus commercials, audiobooks, and video games — including *Strawberry Shortcake* and
*My Little Pony*; live-action debut as **Anika in Prime Video's *The Boys***. **Honors degree
in Political Science, specializing in Political Theory** — "I enjoy reading about the dynamics
of power and why people in positions of power do what they do." League of Legends player,
pianist, recent gym convert. 18K Instagram followers.
**Backstory:** Her mother fought cancer for 18 years and died in 2020 — "the most positive and
optimistic person, despite the pain." She was a big Survivor fan too. "I'm also playing for
her."
**Bio signals:** "Goofy, ambitious, chaotic." Wants Survivor because "who doesn't want to lie
and deceive in a setting where it's totally acceptable?" Identifies with **Tony**; wants to
play like **Ricard** — "severely underrated… a strategy mastermind." Alliance value, stated
with unusual candor: "**If an alliance partner is willing to divulge others' secrets to me, I
feel like they have put their trust in me. Which is quite unfortunate for them, 'cause trusting
me might be a mistake.**"
**Fantasy read:** The most openly predatory alliance answer anyone gave — she is describing a
honeypot strategy in a casting questionnaire. A political theory degree plus two decades of
professional character work is a real toolkit: enormous social range, genuine understanding of
power dynamics. The flip side is that a professional performer who gets clocked as *performing*
loses all trust instantly, and Tony/Ricard is an aggressive comp pairing that invites scrutiny.
**Projection:** Widest distribution on the cast. Winner or episode-4 boot.

### Patt Cannaday, 33 · Federal prosecutor · Washington, DC
**Vitals:** Tampa, FL. Law doctorate, Washington University (2019). Federal prosecutor per CBS;
**rumored-cast reporting had her as a U.S. Navy attorney in Norfolk prosecuting serious
military crimes** — treat the military-JAG detail as unconfirmed, the prosecutor role as
confirmed. Runs a **boxing club for at-risk youth**. Boxing, skydiving, hiking, "dramatic
reading nights." Married to her husband — the accomplishment she names first. **Homeschooled**
by her mother; her father was a **26-year Air Force officer** who, in his own telling,
"succeeded primarily based on his good looks." Says she has never once heard her parents raise
their voice at anyone.
**Bio signals:** "Goofy, loyal, intense." Comps: **Amanda Kimmel** (loyal, resilient, close
allies) and **Rachel LaMont** (scrappy, no-holds-barred) — "both were brilliant and flexible."
Alliance value: "someone willing to take risks with me and engage in shenanigans." On
preparation, a six-hour Brazilian hike that turned into an unplanned night climb with no shared
language: "I'm used to adventures I'm fully unprepared for and get by solely through the power
of friendship." Closer: "my sparkling personality, undeniable wit, and of course, my good
looks." Pet peeve: "when the least informed person is telling everyone else what to do."
**Fantasy read:** Boxing plus skydiving plus prosecutorial discipline is a real package, and
the "get by through the power of friendship" story is the correct instinct for this game.
Two problems: "federal prosecutor" is one of maybe three occupations that gets you targeted on
sight, and that pet peeve describes someone who will visibly correct people who are wrong —
which is exactly how smart players make enemies in week one.
**Projection:** High variance. Intense first-impression players either run the tribe or get run
out of it.

### Devin Way, 34 · Actor · Los Angeles, CA
**Vitals:** Lufkin, TX; born May 31, 1992. Dr. Blake Simms on ABC's *Grey's Anatomy* (S16);
series lead in Peacock's *Queer as Folk* reboot; BET's *Sistas*. Actor and model, 49K
Instagram followers. **One of 13 siblings — he's number 7.** Plays Dungeons & Dragons weekly.
Line dancing, spoken word, comics, karaoke, gym. Grandmother ("MoeMoe") is his inspiration.
**Bio signals:** "Charming, playful, loyal." Wants to play like **Rick Devens** — "his joy,
smile and love for the game." Identifies with **Joe Hunter**: "his integrity and love for his
family mixed with **the grief of losing a sibling tragically**." On preparation: weekly D&D —
"the game teaches you to roll the dice and no matter what happens, with the help of your team,
you can figure it out." Alliance value, rigidly stated: "Loyalty and honesty will keep me. **The
second I find out you lied or betrayed me in some way I'll be looking for a new alliance.**"
Closer: "Because 'Ways Always Win.'"
**Fantasy read:** Two structural problems. First, he is the most recognizable person here and a
working series lead — that invites the "he doesn't need the money" vote, and 49K followers means
some castmates may know his face. Second, and more damaging: treating a single lie as
disqualifying is a losing posture in a season explicitly built on chaos and returning twists —
players who burn an ally over one lie run out of allies. Growing up seventh of thirteen is
genuine social training, and Devens energy earns screen time.
**Projection:** Mid-tier, unpredictable. Entertaining, not a favorite.

### Maggie Nestor, 40 · Farmer / homeschool teacher · Charles Town, WV
**Vitals:** Middleway, WV. **Mother of three**, whom she homeschools; camp counselor and
farmer. **First castaway ever to reside in West Virginia.** Hobby list: farming, gardening,
fishing, hunting, exploring, running, lifting, **pole vaulting**, reading, teaching, cooking,
guitar. Proudest of "the life my husband and I created." Inspiration: "Jesus because He saved
me." Declined to answer the pet peeves question. 861 Instagram followers — the smallest
footprint on the cast.
**Superfan:** Watching since 15 — a 25-year wait, matching Kristin's.
**Bio signals:** "Joyful, intentional, **redneck**." On what prepared her: "Growing up in West
Virginia." Identifies with **Parvati** — "I don't know who I'm going to play most like. We'll
see throughout the season." Wants an ally "creative, open, happy, and willing to make bold
moves."
**Fantasy read:** The most legitimately *survival*-capable person who will step on that beach —
she hunts, fishes, farms, and cooks, and in the first 72 hours that is worth more than any
strategic plan. Pole vaulting at 40 is a real athletic credential (explosive, technical,
requires upper-body strength). Her answers are also the shortest of anyone's, which reads as
either grounded or guarded. Self-identifying with Parvati at 40 signals intent to play hard;
analysts flagged the bold-move instinct as potentially reckless on a 26-day clock.
**Projection:** Survives early on pure utility. Ceiling depends entirely on impulse control.

### Aaliyah Puglia, 25 · Chef · Providence, RI
**Vitals:** Gloucester City, NJ → Miami at 13 "with little warning" → Providence for college,
knowing no one. Johnson & Wales culinary nutrition degree; specializes in plant-based cuisine;
**cooked for the New England Patriots (2024)**. **The first known Survivor contestant raised by
same-sex parents.** Turned 25 on roughly Day 4 of filming.
**Bio signals:** "Confident, wise, sporadic." Identifies with **Yam Yam** — "makes jokes
naturally, can be loud but calculated, always tries to find a way in"; expects to play like
**Cirie** — "calm outside appearance, working multiple angles, considers all options, goes to
bat for her allies." On preparation: a life of forced relocations means "I'm used to being
adaptable, flexible, and for things to change without notice."
**Fantasy read:** A professional chef is the single most valuable camp role in Survivor — rice
management alone buys weeks of goodwill, and it makes you indispensable in a way that isn't
threatening. Yam Yam and Cirie are both winner-adjacent comps and unusually well-chosen for
someone her age. The adaptability answer is grounded in real biography rather than
aspiration. Youngest tier of the cast with a notably mature stated approach.
**Projection:** Sneaky-good. Cooking gets her to the merge; the rest turns on whether "calm,
multiple angles" survives contact.

### Cristian Chavez, 26 · Head of HR · Salt Lake City, UT
**Vitals:** Utah State; **earned his master's degree at 21**. Mr. Congeniality at the Mr. USU
pageant. **A dad** — he flags this himself as something people wouldn't guess. Gem collector.
Baking, philosophy, feeding ducks. Strong aversion to worms. Very loud laugh. **Was an
alternate for Season 47**, so this is his second trip through casting.
**Backstory:** His father ran homemade Survivor-style challenges for him growing up. His mother
is his stated inspiration — "she always chose her kids over everything."
**Bio signals:** "Fun, chaotic, loud." Identifies with **Tai**; wants to play "a weird mix of
Tai, Cirie, Tony and Carolyn." Alliance value: "Someone who thinks differently than me, but can
also hear my side. Predictability — I want to be able to predict their motivation." Closer:
"**Because not only am I willing to do anything to win, but if I'm not winning, I'll do whatever
it takes to make everyone else lose.**"
**Fantasy read:** That closer is the most chaotic sentence in the entire cast packet, and it
sits strangely against the duck-feeding, gem-collecting, Tai-identifying persona — which is
either an excellent disguise or a warning that he'll torch a game out of spite. HR is real
people-reading experience and "predict their motivation" is a genuinely strategic filter. Two
years of prep since the S47 alternate slot means he arrives sharp.
**Projection:** Boom or bust. Entertaining floor, unpredictable ceiling.

## TIER D — Early-exit risk

### Thien An Nguyen, 25 · Medical student · Fort Worth, TX ⬆ *upgraded within tier*
**Vitals:** Anne Burnett Marion School of Medicine at TCU. **5'0"** — the youngest and smallest
player. **Marathon runner** (Houston Marathon 2025, "running a marathon on a whim"). Did **Boy
Scouts as a kid — "yes, Boy Scouts"** — which she names as her survival-skills preparation.
**Back-to-back all-around intramural champion.** Ruthless Catan trader.
**Bio signals:** "Quick-witted, empathetic, outgoing." Identifies with **Kenzie** — "a
firecracker who played a social game close to her heart, yet still strategic." Alliance value:
"PLEASE someone that won't stab me in the back (well, first at least!)." Proudest of a friend
she made during Covid who later wrote her, "You taught me what being happy truly means." Pet
peeves: unfairness, fake people, your/you're. Closer: "Coming in at **5 feet strong**, I will
be LURKING incognito-ly until it's time to strike."
**Fantasy read:** More athletic than her billing — marathon endurance plus a two-time
all-around intramural title plus actual Boy Scout outdoor training is a better package than
"tiny medical student" implies, and endurance matters enormously in Fiji. Kenzie is a winner
comp and the self-awareness is high. The problem is purely structural: 5'0" and visibly the
youngest is the default "weakest link" vote when a tribe loses in week one, and there's no
safety net this season.
**Projection:** Talent well above her draft slot, badly exposed to a bad tribe draw. **Buy
aggressively if she survives the first two tribals.**

### Lewis Kelly, 28 · Farmer / creator · Corozal, PR
**Vitals:** **First Irish-born castaway.** Dublin native; moved to Puerto Rico for his wife,
creator **Andrea Camila**; became a U.S. citizen in 2025. Their joint TikTok has ~7.3M
followers; his personal Instagram is 80K — **the largest social footprint on the cast.** They
renovated a school bus and live in it. **Has visited 40+ countries, including North Korea** —
which he cites as his Survivor preparation: isolated, confined to rooms, no phones, no outside
contact, rock-hard beds, never knowing what tomorrow held, "not knowing if one wrong move is
going to get you… voted out!"
**Bio signals:** "Cheeky, devilish, charming." Favorite hobby, first answer: "**Lying!** What
do you mean I can go on a show where it's okay to deceive people?!" Identifies with **Venus**;
also "a better, cooler version of **Tyson**." Alliance value: "Someone that's there to cause
chaos and have fun. Someone that won't rat me out. **Snitches get their torches snuffed.**"
Self-aware: "I might sound a bit rough around the edges, but every blunder, every mistake I
make publicly is probably very thought out and deliberately executed." Closer, remarkably: "**I
probably won't be the Sole Survivor.** If these players have any notion about them they'll make
sure I don't get to Final Tribal Council."
**Fantasy read:** The charm is real and an Irish accent is a genuine early-game social asset —
the Greg Buis effect. The North Korea answer is the single best "what prepared you" response
anyone gave, and the "my blunders are deliberate" line suggests a smarter player than the
persona. But leading with *lying* as your hobby, comping to Tyson, and openly conceding you'll
be cut before the finale is a profile the tribe will read fast. He is also the one player
castmates might genuinely recognize.
**Projection:** Fun and highly watchable pre-merge; unlikely to last. Late-round flier.

### Rob Antonson, 40 · Airline gate agent · Cumberland, RI
**Vitals:** Johnston, RI. Airport operations for **JetBlue**. Hosts **"The Rob Show"** podcast;
aspiring comedian; does his own video editing. Three young kids, whose sports teams he coaches;
married. Red Sox fan. **A self-described "huge reality TV junkie" — Real Housewives, The
Traitors, Love Island, The Challenge**: "if it's reality, I've probably watched it." IG locked.
**Bio signals:** "Competitive, funny, loving." Leans directly into the **Boston Rob**
comparison — "he's from the New England area and is a Sox fan like myself. He carries himself
with this swagger of not caring what others think, which is exactly how I am!" Will "play a
loyal game like Joe" with "some manipulation like Tony." On preparation: the airport is "full
of chaos… Survivor chaos to me is just another busy Friday." Alliance value: someone who is "an
open book" and "will shoot down my name when talked about from others."
**Fantasy read:** Genuinely likable on paper, and gate agent is elite de-escalation training —
he defuses furious strangers for a living, which is underrated. But walking into a
75%-superfan cast as a 40-year-old New England guy who *volunteers* the Boston Rob comparison
is close to the worst available first impression; that archetype now gets pre-emptively voted
out. His broad reality-TV literacy is a real asset for reading a season's structure, but it
won't help if he's gone on Day 3. Preseason fan reaction has already skewed negative.
**Projection:** First-boot candidate if his tribe loses immunity. Do not draft early.
