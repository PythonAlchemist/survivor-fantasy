# PART 1 — ATHLETIC PERFORMANCE

## Verified competitive records

### Ori Jean-Charles — the real deal, and better than his own bio claims
**Measurables: 6'3", 235 lbs** (Albany roster).
- Recruited out of Spring Valley HS as a **6'1.5", 208 lb dual-threat quarterback** (247Sports)
  — he converted to linebacker in college. QB→LB conversions require top-tier general athleticism.
- **Louisville** (2018) → **Maine** (2019): played *both* linebacker and tight end in all 12
  games — **27 tackles, 2.5 TFL, 1 sack, 1 fumble recovery, 1 interception returned for a TD.**
- **Albany** (2022–23). 2023 was the season: **116 tackles (67 solo, 49 assisted), 5.0 sacks
  across 15 games**, including a **17-tackle game vs. Towson**. Named **All-CAA Second Team
  Defense** and **Phil Steele All-CAA Second Team**, plus the CAA Commissioner's Academic Honor
  Roll. Albany earned the **No. 5 seed in the FCS playoffs** that year.
- ⚠️ **Two claims in his CBS bio don't check out.** He says he "broke an NCAA Division I record"
  — no such record appears in any public database; 116 tackles is an excellent FCS season, not a
  record. He also calls himself a "former pro athlete"; there is a DraftScout profile from the
  2024 cycle but **no evidence of an NFL, UFL, or CFL roster spot.** One report says he had the
  D1 opportunity and walked away. Neither embellishment is disqualifying, but a cast of superfans
  that includes a Yankees executive may well fact-check him.

**Verdict: the best athlete on the beach.** Elite FCS production, positional versatility, and
size. Also the largest post-merge target.

### Brady Booker — pro-wrestler athleticism, oversold football résumé
**Measurables: 6'3", 235 lbs** (South Dakota roster).
- Path: **Iowa Central CC** (redshirt 2018) → **University of South Dakota**. His actual D1
  production is thin: **all 12 games in 2019, but primarily on special teams, with 4 total
  tackles.** ESPN lists him as a defensive lineman. "D1 linebacker" is true on the roster sheet
  and misleading as a performance claim.
- The real credential is **high school**: 4× all-conference, **2× Defensive Player of the Year**,
  and he still holds St. Bede Academy's records for tackles in a game, a season, and a career.
  Also played baseball and basketball.
- **Youth gymnast** — the most underrated line in his bio. Gymnastics is the single best predictor
  of balance-beam and obstacle-course challenge performance.
- Current: **reigning 1FW Heavyweight Champion**, ex-WWE NXT ("Bodhi Hayward," Chase U), AEW Dark
  and Ring of Honor tapings. Weightlifting, trail running, **jujitsu**.

**Verdict: elite power and body control, mediocre football stats.** Pro wrestling requires
strength, agility, pain tolerance, and touring on minimal sleep — which he names himself as his
preparation. The gymnastics background may matter more in Fiji than the football did.

### Patt Cannaday — the biggest upgrade of this research round
- **A certified USA Boxing head coach.** Not a hobbyist: she has coached hundreds of youth at risk
  of gang violence and **taken a competition team to tournaments.**
- LinkedIn confirms the credential chain rumored earlier: **former U.S. Navy JAG, former Assistant
  U.S. Attorney**, now a federal prosecutor. The military background is real, not rumor.
- Also skydives and hikes.

**Verdict: legitimate combat-sport conditioning plus military background.** Boxing means cardio,
grip, core, and — most relevantly — the trained ability to keep making decisions while exhausted
and being hit. Significantly more athletic than "federal prosecutor" implies.

### Eric Macksoud — the best water athlete, hiding in plain sight
**Measurables: 5'10"** (Clark roster).
- **Competitive swimmer from age 8 through his senior year of college** — confirmed on the Clark
  University Men's Swimming & Diving roster, senior in **2012–13**. That's roughly 14 straight
  years of competitive swimming.
- Also trained in **Muay Thai kickboxing.**
- His own framing: *"I don't present as the athletic type, so it does throw some people off when
  they learn about my athletic history."*

**Verdict: the single most schedule-relevant athlete on the cast.** A large share of Fijian
challenges are swim-and-dive — retrieving keys, hauling ropes underwater, ocean starts. A 14-year
competitive swimmer who reads as a soft, chatty therapist is the most favorable combination
available: he wins water challenges without collecting the "physical threat" label that ends
Ori's and Brady's games. **This is the biggest edge in the draft.**

### Kristin Flickinger — a serious endurance athlete, and I undersold her
- Her "road cycling" hobby is connected to her career: she **directed AIDS/LifeCycle**, the
  545-mile, seven-day San Francisco-to-Los Angeles ride, raising **$20M+ for HIV services.**
  Long-distance cycling at 49 means real aerobic base and, more importantly, a trained tolerance
  for sustained discomfort — which is what a 26-day starvation game actually tests.

**Verdict: endurance-strong, power-limited.** Won't win a slugfest; genuinely dangerous in
anything long, and better equipped than most to still be functional on Day 20.

### Thien An Nguyen — small, and the fittest player nobody will respect
**Measurables: 5'0"** (self-reported).
- **Marathon runner** — completed the 2025 Houston Marathon, and describes "running a marathon on
  a whim," which implies a maintained aerobic base rather than a one-off.
- **Back-to-back all-around intramural champion.**
- **Boy Scouts as a kid** — "yes, Boy Scouts" — which she names as her actual outdoor/survival
  preparation. She is the only castaway with formal wilderness training.

**Verdict: badly miscast by appearance.** Marathon endurance plus two all-around titles plus
actual bushcraft is a better package than most of the cast. But 5'0" is 5'0", and tribes vote on
what they see in the first 72 hours.

### Carter Krull — I overrated him; correcting
**Measurables: 5'10", 175 lbs** (Sioux Falls roster).
- **He never appeared in a college game.** Roster freshman in 2021; the 2020 season was cancelled
  for COVID. "Played wide receiver and kicker in college" is a roster fact, not a playing record.
- His **high school** résumé is genuinely strong: multi-sport at Central Lyon (baseball,
  basketball, football, track), wingback/DB/kicker, **65 receptions for 901 yards, 376 rushing
  yards, 104 tackles, 4 INTs**, and a **50.5-yard kickoff average** with 97/111 on PATs. Class 2A
  first-team all-district as a junior and senior, Special Teams MVP, senior captain.
- Daily ranch labor is real functional strength regardless of the stat sheet.

**Verdict: fit and coordinated, but 175 lbs and undersized.** Downgraded from "challenge asset"
to "useful and durable." His value is camp utility and likability, not challenge dominance.

# PART 2 — SOCIAL CHARACTERISTICS

## The headline: this is a professionally-social cast

**Fifteen of the 21 hold jobs whose core competency is managing other people** — attorney,
prosecutor, therapist, HR head, professor, physician, crisis manager, personal trainer/youth
director, chef, gate agent, wedding photographer, actor (×2), pro wrestler, executive negotiator.
That is unusually high, and it means the pre-merge social game will be played at a higher level
than a typical newbie season. Fewer easy targets; more people who know how to be liked on purpose.

### Trained persuaders — people paid to change minds
- **Alexis Levine** — criminal defense attorney. Her framing is the most useful in the packet:
  defense work means "constantly being **on the bottom**" and thinking creatively from there.
  Being down 4-2 after a swap is the modal Survivor position, and she does it professionally.
- **Patt Cannaday** — federal prosecutor, former Navy JAG, former AUSA. The other side of the same
  skill. Also the occupation most likely to get her targeted on sight.
- **Kristin Flickinger** — **the most accomplished coalition-builder on the cast, by a wide
  margin.** Director of Programs at the **Los Angeles LGBT Center**, the world's largest LGBTQ+
  organization, running programs for youth, seniors, women, and trans communities simultaneously.
  Directed AIDS/LifeCycle. Relaunched the Pacific Pride Festival by assembling **40+ partner
  organizations**. Managed the international sunset of major events. Now a professional
  crisis-management consultant and **paid conference speaker**. Getting groups with different
  interests to move together *is her entire career.*
- **Mike Pinsky** — negotiates contracts and roster decisions for the Yankees. His stated alliance
  criterion is pure intelligence-gathering, which is the correct read on what actually drives
  votes.

### Trained observers — people paid to read others
- **Jelly Loblack** — PhD sociologist with formal **ethnographic interviewing and observation**
  training. She is the only person here professionally trained to embed in a group and decode its
  dynamics from the inside. Her pitch — "there's nobody better equipped to win a social experiment
  than the sociologist who creates them" — is arrogant and basically correct.
- **Eric Macksoud** — licensed mental health counselor. Reads affect for a living. Risk: therapists
  can read as *analyzing* you, which breeds unease.
- **Cristian Chavez** — Head of HR. Investigates conflicts, reads motivation, manages people who
  don't want to be managed. His alliance filter — "**predictability, I want to be able to predict
  their motivation**" — is a genuinely strategic criterion.
- **Ana Sani** — political theory honors degree ("why people in positions of power do what they
  do") plus 60+ animated shows of professional character work.
- **Danny Kilby** — ⚠️ co-hosts an **edgic** podcast, the fan discipline of predicting winners from
  editing patterns. He analyzes Survivor narratives as a hobby. His alliance tell — "**I want to
  know someone's real laugh before I lock in, so I know when they're lying in the future**" — is a
  professional-grade calibration technique that most players never think of.

### Rapport-under-pressure — people who defuse strangers for a living
- **Jenna Doore** — produces calm and trust from anxious strangers on the highest-stakes day of
  their lives. Her framing: "it's easy to write me off as the cute little wedding photographer —
  when in reality what I do takes grit, stamina, and mental toughness… the name of the game is
  ADAPTABILITY."
- **Rob Antonson** — airport gate agent at **Logan International** for JetBlue. He de-escalates
  furious strangers all day, every day. His own line: "the airport is full of chaos… Survivor
  chaos to me is just another busy Friday at the airport." Genuinely elite training that his
  archetype problem may never let him use.
- **Linnea Capobianco** — co-owns **Afterglow** with her brother Andrew: **four locations**
  (Chelsea, Williamsburg, Upper East Side, and Paramus NJ). Selling intimate products means
  handling embarrassed customers with total composure — she says the retail experience honed her
  ability to "manage delicate interpersonal exchanges." She is unembarrassable, which is rare and
  useful. Her mother is a scientist, which she credits for her stated top alliance value: rational.
- **Sharonda Cox** — OB-GYN. Delivers hard news and manages people through fear and pain.
- **Aaliyah Puglia** — professional kitchens, plus cooking for the Patriots. Kitchen brigades are
  hierarchical, high-stress, and fast — and camp cooking is the most reliably non-threatening path
  to indispensability in the game.

### Performers — people comfortable being watched
**Devin Way** (series lead), **Ana Sani** (Canadian Screen Award winner), **Eric Macksoud**
(community theater), **Danny Kilby** (improv, karaoke, filmmaking), **Rob Antonson** (aspiring
comedian, host of "The Rob Show"), **Lewis Kelly** (content creator, ~7.3M joint TikTok).
Performers get confessionals and screen time, which matters for airtime-based fantasy scoring
independent of placement.

### Leaders of young people
**Ori Jean-Charles** — Youth Services Director at the MLK Center, "Coach O." **Patt Cannaday** —
USA Boxing head coach for at-risk youth. **Maggie Nestor** — homeschools three children and works
as a camp counselor. **Jelly Loblack** — university professor. All four are practiced at directing
groups without formal authority, which is exactly what alliance leadership requires.

## Social assets that don't show up in a bio

- **Kristin's intergenerational program.** She built and ran a culinary arts program pairing LGBTQ+
  youth with LGBTQ+ seniors, reaching thousands. The central pre-merge problem for a 49-year-old
  is connecting with 25-year-olds — and she has documented, professional, repeated success doing
  exactly that. This meaningfully reduces her age risk and is the reason I've moved her up.
- **Linnea's family secret.** Her parents have hidden her business from their social circle and
  view it unfavorably; going on Survivor exposes it. Someone playing with that at stake plays with
  conviction.
- **Aaliyah's biography of forced adaptation.** NJ → Miami at 13 "with little warning" → Rhode
  Island for college knowing nobody → solo travel for work. Her adaptability claim is grounded in
  lived pattern, not aspiration. She is also the first known contestant raised by same-sex parents.
- **Ori's family narrative.** First in his family to earn a degree, then a master's; sent money
  home rather than asking for it; every sibling has since graduated — "I broke a generational
  curse." That is a jury-winning story if he ever gets to tell it.
- **Grief as fuel.** Mike Pinsky (mother, metastatic breast cancer, he was 24), Ana Sani (mother,
  18-year cancer fight, died 2020, also a Survivor fan), Devin Way (sibling, lost tragically).
  Players carrying a specific person into the game tend not to quit.
- **Two 25-year waits.** Kristin Flickinger has applied since the middle of Season 1; Maggie Nestor
  has wanted this since she was 15. Neither is going to waste it playing scared.
