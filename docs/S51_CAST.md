# Survivor 51 — Cast Reference

**Premiere:** Wednesday, September 23, 2026 (CBS) · **Location:** Mamanuca Islands, Fiji
**Format:** 21 castaways · 26 days · two starting tribes — **Savu** and **Toka** (yellow / purple)
**Branding:** "The Open Era" — any twist or advantage from any past season can return, at any time, in any order, without warning.

## Tribe assignments: NOT YET PUBLIC (as of 2026-09-09)
CBS has confirmed only the two tribe names and colors. Buffs are pre-organized per player and
handed out on the beach ("find your name, grab your buff"), and one player does **not** start
with the others — they leave immediately on a "mysterious journey" (Exile/Journey-style twist).
Rosters should become known at/just before the Sept 23 premiere.
